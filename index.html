HOW TO PLAY

🕵️ THE DETECTIVE (1 Player)

You are Detective Marlowe, trapped inside. Collect all 8 Master Keys to unlock the emergency exit and escape. Avoid the children — they will drain your sanity on contact. Gather fries to restore hunger and sanity.

👦 THE CHILDREN (All Other Players)

You are one of the stranded children, now feral and organized. Hunt the detective together. If any child touches the detective enough to drain their sanity to zero, the children win. Coordinate using the minimap.

⚠ INCIDENT REPORT — CLASSIFIED ⚠

The storm hit on a Tuesday. A school bus carrying seven children from Beachwood Elementary hydroplaned off Route 9 and crashed through the front window of the long-abandoned McDonald's on the edge of town. The driver fled. The phones died. The power went out.

That was three days ago.

At first they cooperated. Then Jack found the manager's keys. Then Roger found the basement. Now they don't want to leave. Now they want to stay — and they want to make sure nobody else leaves either.

You are Detective Marlowe, first officer on scene. Your radio is dead. The exits are locked. And somewhere in the grease-soaked dark, children are waiting.

🎯 OBJECTIVES

DETECTIVE: Collect 8 Master Keys → unlock the emergency exit → escape.

CHILDREN: Hunt the detective together. Drain their sanity to zero before they escape.

Fries restore sanity and hunger. Keys are hidden throughout the restaurant. Coordination wins.
